Academic research profile

Research interests: classical reception, cognitive linguistics, and gender theory.
Francesca's PhD thesis focuses on the interconnections between simile and reception theories, and their workings in several modern receptions of Homer.

Publications

  • Forthcoming: ‘“The helmet screams... across three throusand years”: simile, transformative temporality, and conceptual mutability in Logue's Homer’, Christopher Logue and the Classics (Oxford: Oxford University Press).
  • With Rhys Kaminski-Jones, 'Introduction', Celts, Romans, Britons: Classical and Celtic Influence in the Construction of British Identities (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2020), 1–19.
  • With Rhys Kaminski-Jones (eds.), Celts, Romans, Britons: Classical and Celtic Influence in the Construction of British Identities (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2020).
